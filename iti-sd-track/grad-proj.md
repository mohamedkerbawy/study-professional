# Government Transactions Management System (Mu'aqib Online)

## Overview

**Mu'aqib Online** is a web-based government transaction management system developed as a graduation project for a Professional Diploma in Software Development. The project was designed for a client who owned a government transaction services office (Mu'aqib office) in Saudi Arabia, with the aim of improving and modernizing the way the office managed and followed up on government transactions.

The system aims to modernize the traditional *Mu'aqib* service model by providing customers with an online platform to request, manage, and track government transactions remotely. This helps reduce the time and effort required to visit government offices and provides customers with easier access to transaction information and status updates.

The project was inspired by the traditional desktop-based *Mu'aqib* applications available at the time, which primarily focused on managing transactions locally and provided limited capabilities for submitting or tracking transactions online. **Mu'aqib Online** extends this concept by introducing web-based access and online transaction tracking.

The system targets government-related transaction services in Saudi Arabia, including Passport and Immigration Services (Jawazat), Labor and Employment Services, Municipal Services, and other government departments.

## Project Context

The system was developed as a **client-oriented graduation project**, based on the requirements of an actual Mu'aqib office. The project involved analyzing the client's business processes and translating them into a web-based software solution designed to improve transaction management, customer communication, and service tracking.

## System Modules

### 1. User Management
- User registration and authentication
- Customer profile management
- User account management

### 2. Customer Management
- Manage individual and business customers
- Customer information and documents
- Customer transaction history

### 3. Government Services Management
- Define available government services
- Manage service requirements
- Configure required documents and procedures

### 4. Transaction Management
- Create new transaction requests
- Assign transactions to service providers
- Manage transaction information
- Upload required documents
- Track transaction status

### 5. Transaction Tracking
- View transaction progress
- Track transaction status
- View transaction history
- Notify users of status changes

### 6. Document Management
- Upload and manage required documents
- Associate documents with transactions
- Maintain transaction documentation

### 7. Administration
- Manage users and system accounts
- Manage government services
- Monitor transactions
- Manage system configuration

### 8. Reports
- Transaction reports
- Customer reports
- Service statistics
- Transaction status reports
- Transaction history
